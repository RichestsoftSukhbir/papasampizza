Meet Papa Sam

A neighbourhood pizzeria with an American soul.

Papa Sam Pizza started with one idea: pizza should taste like someone cared. So we knead our dough daily, simmer our sauce slowly and pile on toppings until the slice sags. Located at CP Square, TDI City, we serve Mohali and Kharar with pizzas, garlic breads and more.
