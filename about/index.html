Who we are

One oven. One promise: make it worth the wait.

Papa Sam Pizza is a family-style pizzeria at CP Square in Sector 117, TDI City, Mohali. Our sign says it plainly — Authentic American Taste — and we mean it: thick, cheesy, generously loaded pizzas that feel like a night at a classic American pizza joint, with flavours our Punjab customers actually love.

We serve Italian, North Indian and American favourites — from pizzas and garlic breads to tikka, keema and more. Everything is prepared fresh, so tell us how you like it and we'll tailor it.
